# How to Check Your PMAFI Membership Status

You can check your membership on the Foundation's website at any time. It takes
about a minute, and there is no password to remember.

---

## 1. Go to the membership page

Visit **www.pmafi.org/membership**

Or, from anywhere on the site, use the menu at the top: **Membership → Check My
Status**.

The check is the first thing on the page, under the heading *"Already a
Member?"*

---

## 2. Look yourself up

There are two ways, and either works:

- **By email address** — the address on your membership. This is the more
  reliable of the two.
- **By name** — your full name, if you are not sure which address the
  Foundation has on file.

Type it in and press the button. That is the whole process.

---

## 3. What the result means

**Active**
Your membership is in good standing. There is nothing you need to do.

**Being verified**
Your application and payment have reached the Foundation, and the team is
checking your receipt. Your membership activates as soon as that is done.
*If you have not sent your receipt yet, email it in — your payment cannot be
confirmed without it.*

**Lapsed**
The Foundation has your membership on record, but it needs renewing. Get in
touch and PMAFI will help you put it right.

**Not found**
See the next section.

---

## If it says your membership was not found

This usually means one of three things:

1. **The Foundation has a different email address for you.** Try looking
   yourself up by name instead.
2. **Your name is recorded differently** — a middle initial, a maiden or married
   name, a different spelling. Try your email address instead.
3. **You have not yet registered through the online form.** Membership records
   are still being moved onto the website, so a long-standing member may not
   appear yet.

**If you believe there is a mistake, contact the Foundation and they will look
it up for you.** Please do not pay the membership fee a second time — get in
touch first.

---

## Your privacy

Only your own record is ever shown. The member list is never published on the
website, and looking yourself up tells you nothing about anybody else.

---

## Getting your digital member ID

Once your membership shows as confirmed, you can create a digital PMAFI ID card:

1. Go to **www.pmafi.org/membership/id**
2. Enter the same email address on your membership
3. Add a photo
4. Download the card and keep it on your phone

Your name, category, standing and PMA class come from the Foundation's own
records, so the card always matches them. Your photo never leaves your device —
it is not uploaded or stored anywhere.
