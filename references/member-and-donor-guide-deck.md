# Slide deck source — How to Become a Member · How to Donate

A walkthrough for **alumni, friends and prospective donors**, written to be
turned into slides. Each slide gives you the words for the slide itself, the
screenshot to take, and what to say over it.

**Everything here describes what the site actually does today**, with two
exceptions marked **⚠ NEEDS CONTENT** — those slides cannot be screenshotted
truthfully until PMAFI fills in the dues and payment details. Take those two
shots last.

Screenshots: use a desktop browser at roughly 1440px wide, and crop to the
section rather than the whole page. Consistent crops make the deck feel made
rather than assembled.

# Links to put on the slides

**Link the website page, not the Google Form.** The form addresses are long,
unmemorable, and — for the donation form especially — designed to be swappable:
its link lives in the content sheet precisely so PMAFI can replace the form
without a developer. A deck that hardcodes it breaks silently the day that
happens. Every page below carries the current button.

| Where | Link | Use it on |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `www.pmafi.org` | Slides 1, 18 |
| Membership | `www.pmafi.org/membership` | Slides 2–8 |

`/about#board` — so an older printed link is safe to quote.

## Two questions your audience will ask

**"Why do I have to pay before applying?"**
So there is no invoice to wait for. Everything arrives in one submission and the
Foundation can verify it immediately.

**"Why does the membership form need a Google account when the donation form
doesn't?"**
Only the membership form accepts a file upload, and Google requires a sign-in
for that. The donation form deliberately has none — PMAFI's own bank record is
the proof there, so nothing needs to be attached.
