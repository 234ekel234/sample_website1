# Build me a 5-slide PowerPoint shell, and the script to read

I am making a narrated walkthrough of a website. I will record the screen and
the voice-over myself — **do not give me recording instructions, shot lists, or
production advice.**

I need two things:

1. **A `.pptx` file, 5 slides.** Each slide carries one short headline and a
   large empty area where I will drop in a video. Nothing else on the slide — no
   bullet lists, no body copy, no icons, no placeholder images. A board that is
   reading cannot listen.
2. **The script for each slide**, written to be read aloud over the video. Put
   each slide's script in that slide's **speaker-notes field**, and also print
   all five scripts in your reply so I can read from them while recording.

Everything you need is in this brief. Do not invent figures, names, dates or
claims that are not written here — this is a real organisation and the deck will
be shown to its trustees. Five slides, and only these five: no caveats slide, no
requests slide, no next-steps slide, no closing slide, however natural they feel.

---

## Audience and tone

The **Board of Trustees** of a non-profit foundation. Senior, mostly retired
general officers. They are not technical.

Tone: plain, confident, unhurried. No marketing language, no exclamation marks,
no "revolutionary" or "seamless" or "empower". State things and stop. British
spelling. This is a report to a board, not a pitch.

The script is spoken, so write it to be *said*: short sentences, no parentheses,
no bullet fragments, and numbers written the way a person reads them aloud
("three thousand pesos", "a hundred and sixty-one names"). About **140 words per
minute** — use that to hit the per-slide timings below.

---

## Background facts — use only these

**The organisation.** The Philippine Military Academy Foundation, Inc. (PMAFI) —
a non-stock, non-profit foundation supporting the Philippine Military Academy at
Fort del Pilar, Baguio City. Registered with the Securities and Exchange
Commission in 1988. Governed by a 15-trustee board plus the PMA Superintendent
ex officio.

**The website.** Live at `www.pmafi.org`. Pages: home, about, programmes,
membership, donate, donation impact, contact.

**Home page.** Introduces the Foundation and what it funds; carries the
Foundation's adopted Vision and Mission, photographs of its work, and a news and
announcements section that staff post to themselves.

**Programmes page.** Sets out the Foundation's work, and publishes the roll of
endowed chairs in full — **161 names**, with the Board's consent, given August
2026.

**Membership.** Roll of **7,747 members**. A **one-time lifetime fee of ₱3,000**,
the same for all three categories (Regular, Associate, Affiliate); categories
describe a person's relationship to the Academy, not an amount. **28 PMA classes
are at 100% membership.**

**Checking your standing.** A member types their **name** — no account, no
password, no membership number, no email address. The matching tolerates a
missing middle name, a different word order, and ordinary misspellings, because
the roll was typed by hand. Where two members share a name, it asks for a PMA
class year rather than listing candidates. **Why name-first matters:** most of
the 7,747-strong roll has never given the Foundation an email address, so
anything requiring one would have excluded almost all of them.

**Digital member ID.** Once found, a member generates a card bearing the
Foundation's seal. Name, category, PMA class and standing come from the
Foundation's own records and **cannot be edited by the member**. The only thing
the member supplies is their photograph, and it **never leaves their own device**
— never uploaded, never stored on a server. The card states the member's standing
**as of the day it was generated**, printed on purpose so an old card does not go
on asserting something that may since have changed.

**Giving.** A Metrobank account and a GCash channel are published on the site. A
donor reports their gift through a short form; staff check it against the
Foundation's own bank record — a person still decides the money arrived — and the
donor is issued a reference code. With that code the donor can look up their own
giving at any time and see recent updates from the fund they gave to.

**Published 2025 figures** (counts only — **never state peso amounts**):
160 professorial chairs funded · 109 books · 21 course directors supported ·
12 faculty recognised · 5 cadets on full scholarship · 8 sent to seminars.

## Rules for the script

- **Never frame anything as a before-and-after, and never say members previously
  had nothing.** The audience is the Board; that reads as a criticism of their
  own stewardship. Say what is there now.
- **Do not mention the Chairman's or President's message** on the home page.
- **No claim that donations are tax deductible.** Nothing on the site says so.
- Do not describe the site as using artificial intelligence.
- Say "PMA class", not "batch". Say "the roll", not "the database".
- Do not name or quote any real member.

---

## Slide design

- **Navy `#1B2A4A`** and **gold `#C8A951`**. Navy background, white headline,
  gold for emphasis only — an accent, never a block fill behind text.
- The headline sits in the upper portion of the slide. Everything below it is
  clear space for the video — a 16:9 area, as large as the layout allows.
- Large type. Nothing below about 28pt.
- No stock photography, no icon grids, no bullet lists, no footers, no slide
  numbers.
