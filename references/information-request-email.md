## Notes for you, not for them

**Send it in one message.** Three separate emails invites three people to assume
someone else is handling it. One message with named sections makes each person's
part unambiguous while everyone can see the whole.

**The address question needs a decision, not a lookup.** Both appear in PMAFI's
own materials, so whoever answers has to choose.

**Dues and payment details are the same unlock.** One dues figure plus one
payment channel switches over both the donate page and the membership page —
worth saying if Finance and the Membership Director start pointing at each other.

**Nothing here asks for anything already supplied.** Membership categories,
programs, mission and vision, the board list, and the roster mechanism are all
done — asking again invites a re-litigation you don't need.

**If a reply is partial, take it.** Bank details alone are worth more than a
complete answer three weeks later.
